About

I'm a technology journalist covering cryptocurrency, AI, quantum computing and robotics—and the people who build, regulate, believe in and are harmed by them.

Previously a reporter at Decrypt, I now publish Exploration Arcanum and its news section, Crypto Insights Journal, exploring where technology meets myth, magick, religion and metaphysics. I'm available for staff or freelance reporting, production and hosting roles.
