I’m a technology journalist covering cryptocurrency, artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and robotics, with a focus on the people those technologies affect: the victims, the believers, the regulators, and the builders.

Previously a reporter at Decrypt, I now run Exploration Arcanum, where I report on where technology meets myth, magick, religion, and metaphysics, and Crypto Insights Journal, its news section. I’m looking for my next role—staff or freelance—and I’m open to work that brings reporting, production, hosting, and audience-building together.
